WHAT DEVICES DO YOU USE?

HOW LONG HAVE YOU BEEN DOING ART?
I've been drawing since I can remember, which my consciousness starts in around 2004. Graphic design, motion graphics, and video editing I started to dabble in around 2012-2013.

I have worked with some notable musicians and bands, such as Shayley Bourget from DayShell, Vans Warped Tour, Kayla Korpics, and more. I have also worked with the county school system local to my high school for yearbook designs and a library logo redesign.
